# What ICT Means to Me
To me, a mechatronics engineering student, Information and Communication Technology is the backbone and the central intelligence behind all sorts of modern tech and automation. ICT is the connecting bridge between software and hardware. It connects systems seamlessly, allowing them to work as an independent and intelligent unit that can utilize the resources of the system and efficiently complete tasks. Mastering and educating myself about ICT means that I can create a system that isn't only a piece of metal, but an intelligent system that can easily solve the problem at hand and stay functional, reliable, and connected. This is why ICT is extremely valued to me and is just as important as creating the physical system.
